# Reviewer notes: contact import flow

- **Task fit:** does each step do one job, and is it clear where Dana is and what comes next?
- **Discoverability:** can Dana see how to go back, change a mapping, fix or skip a problem row?
- **Hierarchy:** on the problems step, is the reason each row fails as visible as the row itself?
- **Regression risk and restraint:** is the irreversible import described plainly before confirming (how many created, updated, skipped)? Is anything ambiguous about what will change?
- **States:** does the network error say what was imported, what was not, and how to continue? Does the complete state give a useful summary?
- **Mobile usability:** at 390 px, are the mapping and problem tables usable without losing the column they describe?
