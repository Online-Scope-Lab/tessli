# Reviewer notes: order exceptions queue

- **Density and hierarchy:** can Joel scan and compare twelve orders quickly: aligned values, consistent columns, the reason held and the delivery date easy to read? Do urgent and old orders stand out without everything shouting?
- **Discoverability:** are search, filter, sort, selection and the bulk actions easy to find and understand?
- **Task fit:** can he open an order and come back to the same place in the list?
- **Regression risk:** is cancelling protected from accidents? Is it clear what a bulk action will do, and to how many orders?
- **Mobile usability:** at 390 px, is the queue still workable (not just a squeezed desktop table)?
- **States:** no results, loading, error and an empty queue each say something specific and offer the right action.
