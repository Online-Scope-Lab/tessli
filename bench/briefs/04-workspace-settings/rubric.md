# Reviewer notes: workspace settings

- **Task fit and discoverability:** can Tomás find each area quickly? Is it obvious how to invite, change a role and remove a member?
- **Hierarchy:** is deleting the workspace clearly separated from everyday settings, and impossible to trigger by accident?
- **Consistency:** do save feedback, errors and confirmations behave the same way across sections?
- **Regression risk:** is it always clear what is saved and what is not? Could Tomás leave with unsaved changes without knowing?
- **States:** does the Agent view make the permission difference understandable without showing controls that do nothing? Does the delete confirmation name what will be lost, and does the failure say that nothing was deleted?
- **Mobile usability:** at 390 px, is the member list readable and are role changes still possible?
