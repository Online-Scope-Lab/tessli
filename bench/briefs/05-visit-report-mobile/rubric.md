# Reviewer notes: mobile visit report

- **Mobile usability (weigh this most):** at 390 px, can Priya complete the report with one hand: large controls, the submit action easy to reach, nothing cramped side by side?
- **Task fit:** is the job and site confirmation quick, and does the outcome choice drive what is required next?
- **Hierarchy:** is it always clear what is still missing before submitting?
- **States:** can Priya tell whether the report was sent or only saved on the phone? Does the sync failure say the report is safe and how to retry?
- **Regression risk:** on the 1440 px screenshots, is the same workflow still usable, or is it stretched or broken?
