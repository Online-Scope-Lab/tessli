# Reviewer notes: landing page

What to look for in this brief, beyond the twelve dimensions' general meaning:

- **Task fit:** within the first screen, can you tell what Paneline does, who it is for and what to do next? Is the workflow (pull request → rendered changes → approval) shown, not just named?
- **Hierarchy:** is there one obvious next action (join the waitlist), or do several actions compete?
- **Mobile usability:** at 390 px, is the menu usable and is the waitlist form easy to reach and complete?
- **Restraint and coherence:** does the page use only the facts in the brief? Count any invented customers, logos, numbers or quotes against it.
- **States:** does the waitlist error say what to fix, and does the success state say what happens next?
