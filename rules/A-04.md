---
id: A-04
category: accessibility
title: Label every control
statement: Give every control a visible label, groups a legend, and errors a link to their field.
why: >-
  Placeholders disappear as soon as people type and are not reliably
  announced. Visible labels, legends and linked errors tell everyone what a
  control is and what went wrong.
check: Clicking a label focuses its control; groups have a legend; errors use aria-describedby.
anti_pattern: >-
  Placeholder-only inputs, a row of radio buttons with no group label, and one
  error banner at the top of the form.
status: draft

reviewed_at: null
sources:

  - WCAG 2.2 SC 3.3.2 Labels or Instructions (A)
