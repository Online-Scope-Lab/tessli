---
id: W-03
category: copy
title: No invented proof
statement: Show no ratings, counts, logos, quotes or metrics you can't source.
why: >-
  Invented proof is false the moment the component ships with it, and
  placeholder claims are routinely left in. Honest UI states only what is
  known.
check: Trace every number, logo and quote to a real source, or label it fictional demo content.
anti_pattern: >-
  "Trusted by 10,000+ teams", a row of placeholder logos, five-star ratings
  and testimonials from people who don't exist.
