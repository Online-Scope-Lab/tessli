---
id: ST-04
category: states
title: Errors explain and offer a way out
statement: Say what failed, what is still safe, and one way forward (retry, edit or contact).
why: >-
  An error without its consequences leaves people worried about their work;
  one without an action leaves them stuck. Both lead to repeated attempts and
  support requests.
check: "Trigger each error: the text names the failure, the impact and an action."
anti_pattern: >-
  A red toast reading "Something went wrong" with no detail and no retry.
