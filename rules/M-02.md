---
id: M-02
category: motion
title: Respect reduced motion
statement: Under prefers-reduced-motion, remove non-essential transforms and transitions.
why: >-
  For people with vestibular disorders, movement on screen can cause dizziness
  and nausea. The operating-system setting is how they ask for less.
check: "Emulate prefers-reduced-motion: reduce; only essential motion remains."
anti_pattern: >-
  Slide-ins, parallax and animated counters that run whatever the
  reduced-motion setting says.
status: draft
