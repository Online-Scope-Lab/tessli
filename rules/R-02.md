---
id: R-02
category: responsive
title: Reflow at 320 without sideways scroll
statement: Fit every surface into 320 CSS px with no sideways scroll; only tables and code may scroll.
why: >-
  320 px is a 1280 px screen at 400% zoom, and the width of small phones.
  Scrolling sideways to read each line makes the page unusable for people who
  zoom.
check: At 320 px, the document is no wider than the viewport and no text is clipped.
anti_pattern: >-
  Fixed min-widths, long unbroken URLs or a row of nowrap buttons that pushes
  the page wider than the screen.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.10 Reflow (AA)
  - "docs/quality-gates.md (Widths: 320px gate)"
---
