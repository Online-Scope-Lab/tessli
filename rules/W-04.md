---
id: W-04
category: copy
title: Answer first
statement: Lead answers, errors and notices with the answer or consequence, then the detail.
why: >-
  People scan. When the first words are the answer ("Yes.", "Nothing was
  deleted.") most readers can stop there; the detail is for those who need it.
check: "Read only the first sentence of each answer or message: it gives the answer or outcome."
anti_pattern: >-
  FAQ answers that open with "Great question! At Acme we believe…" and errors
  that start with an apology and bury what happened.
