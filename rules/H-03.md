---
id: H-03
category: hierarchy
title: One eyebrow at most
statement: Use at most one uppercase eyebrow per page, and only if it adds information.
why: >-
  A tracked-out label over every heading adds a layer of hierarchy that
  carries no information, and it is one of the most recognisable marks of
  generated UI. A heading that makes its point needs no label.
check: "Count uppercase, letter-spaced labels over headings: one per page at most."
anti_pattern: >-
  A small accent-coloured "FEATURES", "PRICING" or "FAQ" label above every
  section heading.
