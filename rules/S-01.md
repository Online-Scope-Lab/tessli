---
id: S-01
category: spacing
title: Group by proximity
statement: Keep space inside a group smaller than space between groups, at every width.
why: >-
  Space is how people see what belongs together. When a heading sits as far
  from its paragraph as from the previous section, the structure has to be
  read instead of seen.
check: "Measure gaps at 1440 and 390: label to content < item to item < section to section."
anti_pattern: >-
  One uniform gap applied to everything, so headings float between sections
  instead of attaching to their content.
