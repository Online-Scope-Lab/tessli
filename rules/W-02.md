---
id: W-02
category: copy
title: Action labels name the outcome
statement: Label actions with what happens next, not "Get started", "Submit" or "Learn more".
why: >-
  Specific labels let people predict the result before they commit, and they
  make sense to screen-reader users who list links and buttons out of context.
check: "Read each button and link out of context: it says what it does or where it goes."
anti_pattern: >-
  "Get started" on every pricing card, "Submit" on every form, and rows of
  "Learn more" links.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context) (A)
  - WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.6 Headings and Labels (AA)
  - "docs/v1.2/plan.md §6.2 (example default: the same CTA label on every card)"
