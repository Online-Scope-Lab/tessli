---
id: R-03
category: responsive
title: Break where the content breaks
statement: Change layout where the content stops fitting, not at device names.
why: >-
  Screens come in every width. A layout that changes where its content needs
  room works at all of them; one tuned to "tablet" and "phone" breaks in
  between.
check: "Resize from 1440 to 320: no width crushes columns or orphans one card on a row."
anti_pattern: >-
  Three pricing cards kept side by side down to 768px because that is the
  tablet breakpoint, squeezing each one to a narrow strip.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - design.md §12 (breakpoints are behaviour triggers, not device labels)
  - "Review dimension: mobile usability (docs/quality-gates.md)"
