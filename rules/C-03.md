---
id: C-03
category: colour
title: The accent is scarce
statement: Spend the primary colour on the primary action and real status, not decoration.
why: >-
  An accent works because it is rare. When icons, headings, borders and
  backgrounds all use it, nothing stands out and the primary action loses its
  pull.
check: At 1440 and 390, every element in the primary colour is the primary action or a status.
anti_pattern: >-
  Primary-coloured icons on every card, tinted section backgrounds, gradient
  headings and accent-coloured text everywhere.
