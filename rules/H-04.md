---
id: H-04
category: hierarchy
title: Numbers only for sequences
statement: Use numbered markers (01, 02, Step 1) only for steps people follow in order.
why: >-
  Numbers promise an order. On things that can be read in any order they add
  noise and send readers looking for a progression that isn't there.
check: "For each numbered marker: does order matter to the user? If not, drop the numbers."
anti_pattern: >-
  Three feature cards labelled 01, 02 and 03 that could be read in any order.
status: draft
