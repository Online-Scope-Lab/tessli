---
id: W-01
category: copy
title: Headings make a specific point
statement: Write headings that make the product's point, not labels like "Features" or "FAQ".
why: >-
  A category label tells people what kind of section they are in, which they
  can already see. A specific heading tells them why to keep reading and still
  makes sense out of context.
check: "Swap in another product's name: if the heading still works, rewrite it."
anti_pattern: >-
  "Features", "Why choose us?", "Frequently Asked Questions" and "Everything
  you need to succeed".
