---
id: C-02
category: colour
title: Never colour alone
statement: Pair every colour-coded meaning (error, selected, status) with text or shape.
why: >-
  Colour alone fails people with colour-vision deficiencies, monochrome
  screens and forced-colours mode. A text label also gives screen readers the
  meaning.
check: "View it in greyscale: every state is still identifiable from text or shape."
anti_pattern: >-
  A red border as the only sign of an error, or a highlighted card as the only
  sign of the recommended plan.
status: draft

reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1 Use of Color (A)
  - "docs/v1.2/plan.md §6.4 (sign-in default: colour-only feedback)"
