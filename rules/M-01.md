---
id: M-01
category: motion
title: Motion only explains change
statement: Animate only changes people cause (open, close, switch); no decorative motion.
why: >-
  Motion that follows an action shows what changed and where. Motion for its
  own sake competes with the content, delays reading and can make people feel
  unwell.
check: Without interaction nothing moves, on load or scroll; transitions take 240ms or less.
anti_pattern: >-
  Floating hero blobs, a fade-up on every section as it scrolls in, parallax
  backgrounds and cursor-follow effects.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - "design.md §10 (motion: 120–240ms; no cursor-follow or scroll-jacking)"
  - design.md §15 (animation for decoration alone)
  - WCAG 2.2 SC 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (A)
