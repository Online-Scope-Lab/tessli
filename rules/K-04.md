---
id: K-04
category: components
title: No identical card grids
statement: Shape each block by its content; don't force unlike content into identical icon cards.
why: >-
  Identical icon cards make every point look equally important and equally
  vague. Evidence, comparisons, questions and steps each have a natural shape,
  and using it shows the content is real.
check: Every grid of 3+ same-shaped cards holds true peers; otherwise use a list or table.
anti_pattern: >-
  Three rounded cards with an icon, a two-word title and a line of filler
  each, for points that have nothing in common.

  - "AGENTS.md §13, v1.1 (tag v1.1-archive): no oversized rounded wrappers as a substitute for hierarchy"
