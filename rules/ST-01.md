---
id: ST-01
category: states
title: Every data region has its states
statement: Design empty, loading and error states for every region that loads or lists data.
why: >-
  Real data is often missing, slow or failing, especially on first use. A
  region designed only for the happy path shows a blank box or a broken layout
  exactly when people need guidance.
check: "Force no data, a slow network and a failed request: each shows a designed state."
anti_pattern: >-
  A table or list that renders nothing, or spins forever, when the data is
  empty or the request fails.
