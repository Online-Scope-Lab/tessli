---
id: S-03
category: spacing
title: Density fits the task
statement: Make data surfaces compact and reading surfaces roomy; don't use one density everywhere.
why: >-
  Data is scanned and compared, so every extra pixel of padding hides a row.
  Marketing and reading surfaces are read once and need air. One density
  everywhere fails one of the two.
check: At 1440×900, data rows are 48px tall or less; reading text has line-height 1.5 or more.
anti_pattern: >-
  Data tables with card-sized rows and generous padding, or landing sections
  packed edge to edge like a spreadsheet.
