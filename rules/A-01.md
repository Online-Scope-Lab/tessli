---
id: A-01
category: accessibility
title: 44px touch targets
statement: Make every interactive target at least 44×44 CSS px; inline text links excepted.
why: >-
  Small targets cause mis-taps on touch screens and are hard to hit with a
  tremor or limited dexterity. WCAG's AA minimum is 24px; 44px is its enhanced
  level and the bar these rules use.
check: At 390, every button, link and control's hit area is 44×44px or more, inline links aside.
anti_pattern: >-
  32px icon buttons, tiny close crosses and tightly packed pill filters.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:

  - "WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.5 Target Size (Enhanced) (AAA: 44px)"
  - "docs/quality-gates.md (Accessibility: touch targets about 44×44px)"
