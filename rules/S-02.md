---
id: S-02
category: spacing
title: One shared edge
statement: Align a section's heading, text and actions on one left edge; centre only short blocks.
why: >-
  A shared edge gives the eye one line to scan down. Centring everything
  starts every line at a different place, which slows reading and leaves long
  text and lists ragged on both sides.
check: At 1440, heading, text and actions share a left edge; centred text runs 3 lines at most.
anti_pattern: >-
  Every section centred: centred heading, centred paragraph, centred cards and
  centred buttons, whatever their length.
