---
id: ST-03
category: states
title: State changes don't move the page
statement: Reserve space so loading, toggles and validation don't shift surrounding content.
why: >-
  Content that jumps makes people lose their place and click the wrong thing.
  Reserving room for text that appears keeps the page still.
check: "Switch every state at 1440 and 390: nothing else moves (CLS under 0.1)."
anti_pattern: >-
  Error text inserted above a field that pushes the form down, or a billing
  note that appears only for annual billing and shifts the buttons.
