---
id: H-01
category: hierarchy
title: One primary action per surface
statement: Give each section, card or dialog at most one primary-styled action.
why: >-
  When two actions share the strongest style, people stop to compare them and
  neither reads as the next step. One primary action makes the intended path
  obvious; everything else becomes secondary or a link.
check: "Count primary-styled actions per section and dialog at 1440 and 390: one at most."
anti_pattern: >-
  A hero with two equally filled buttons ("Get started" and "Book a demo"), or
  the same filled button repeated on every card in a grid.
