---
id: A-06
category: accessibility
title: Keyboard complete, nothing hover-only
statement: Make everything work by keyboard, close overlays on Escape, and hide nothing behind hover.
why: >-
  Many people navigate by keyboard or switch device, and touch screens have no
  hover. Anything reachable only by mouse or hover is missing for them.
check: Keyboard alone reaches and operates everything; Escape closes overlays and focus returns.
anti_pattern: >-
  Menus that open only on hover, tooltips holding essential information, and
  dialogs you can't leave by keyboard or Escape, or that drop focus on close.
