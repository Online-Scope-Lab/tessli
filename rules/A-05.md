---
id: A-05
category: accessibility
title: Announce changes in place
statement: Announce results, saves and errors that appear in place through a polite live region.
why: >-
  Sighted people see a result count change or a "Saved" message; screen-reader
  users hear nothing unless the change is announced.
check: "With a screen reader, filter, save and fail: each change is announced once, focus unmoved."
anti_pattern: >-
  A result count that updates silently, or a toast that appears and disappears
  without being announced.
status: draft

reviewed_at: null
