---
id: R-01
category: responsive
title: Recompose for small screens
statement: On phones, restack content by priority and adapt components; don't shrink the desktop.
why: >-
  A scaled-down desktop keeps the desktop's order and density on a screen a
  third as wide. Recomposing puts the most important content and action first,
  at a usable size.
check: "At 390: priority order holds, nothing is cramped side by side, text is no smaller."
anti_pattern: >-
  Three columns squeezed side by side on a phone, or a desktop table shrunk to
  tiny text.
