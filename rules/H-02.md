---
id: H-02
category: hierarchy
title: Headings follow the outline
statement: One h1 per page; nest h2–h4 in order. Level follows structure, size follows style.
why: >-
  Screen-reader users move through a page by its headings, so the outline is
  its table of contents. Choosing a level for its default size breaks that
  outline and makes a component wrong as soon as it is reused elsewhere.
check: "Run axe heading-order or list the outline: one h1, no skipped levels."
anti_pattern: >-
  Every section title an h2 regardless of nesting, card titles that jump from
  h2 to h5 to get a smaller font, or styled divs standing in for headings.
