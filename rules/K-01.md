---
id: K-01
category: components
title: Native elements first
statement: Use the native element (button, a, details, input, select, dialog) before a custom widget.
why: >-
  Native elements come with keyboard support, focus handling, form behaviour
  and screen-reader semantics. A custom widget has to rebuild all of that and
  usually misses some of it.
check: No clickable div or span (onClick, role="button"); Tab reaches every control.
anti_pattern: >-
  A div with onClick as a button, a script-driven accordion instead of details
  and summary, a custom toggle that isn't a radio or checkbox.
