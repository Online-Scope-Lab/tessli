---
id: W-05
category: copy
title: Arrows only for leaving
statement: Put an arrow on a link only when it leaves the site (↗), never on every link.
why: >-
  An arrow on everything stops meaning anything. Kept for links that take
  people elsewhere, it warns them before they click.
check: Every link or button with an arrow glyph or icon goes to another site or app.
anti_pattern: >-
  "Learn more →" on every card, buttons with trailing arrows, and arrow icons
  on in-page actions.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - "docs/v1.2/plan.md §4 (tells: arrows only where they mean \"goes somewhere else\")"
  - design.md §14 (tells to avoid)
