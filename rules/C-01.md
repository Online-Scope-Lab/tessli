---
id: C-01
category: colour
title: Semantic tokens only
statement: Colour with semantic tokens (background, primary, muted, border…), never raw values.
why: >-
  Raw colours lock a component to one palette and break dark mode and the
  installing project's theme. Semantic roles let the same structure work in
  any theme.
check: No hex, rgb(), hsl() or palette classes in the component; it works in light and dark.
anti_pattern: >-
  Hard-coded hex values, Tailwind palette classes such as text-gray-500 and
  white cards that ignore the project's theme and vanish in dark mode.
