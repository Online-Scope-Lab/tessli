---
id: T-01
category: typography
title: Readable line length
statement: Cap running text at a readable measure (about 65ch), never the full container width.
why: >-
  Long lines make the eye lose its place on the return sweep; very short ones
  break phrases apart. A fixed measure keeps text readable however wide the
  screen is.
check: At 1440, every body paragraph has a max-width of 80ch or less.
anti_pattern: >-
  A paragraph stretched across a 1280px container, or a hero description
  inheriting the full page width.
status: draft
reviewed_by: []
reviewed_at: null
sources:
  - design.md §5 (paragraph measure 58–68ch)
  - "WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.8 Visual Presentation (AAA: no more than 80 characters)"
